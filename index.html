CalBar Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

CalBar is a macOS menu bar app that displays the progress of your current Calendar event and shows upcoming events and reminders. This Privacy Policy explains how CalBar handles your data.

Data Collection

CalBar does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data.

CalBar does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Calendar and Reminders Access

CalBar requests access to your Calendar and Reminders only to display your current event, upcoming events, all-day events, and today's reminders in the macOS menu bar.

Calendar and Reminders data is read locally on your Mac through Apple's EventKit framework. This data never leaves your device.

Network Transmission

CalBar does not upload your Calendar or Reminders data to any server. CalBar does not send your schedule, event titles, reminder titles, notes, locations, or other calendar-related information over the Internet.

Local Processing

All calendar and reminder processing is performed locally on your Mac. CalBar only uses this information to display schedule progress and related menu bar information.

Third-Party Services

CalBar does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

CalBar does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
