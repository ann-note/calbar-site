CalBar

Calendar progress in your Mac menu bar.

CalBar is a macOS menu bar app that shows the progress of your current calendar event directly in the menu bar area.

It helps you stay aware of meetings, focus blocks, classes, and reminders without opening Calendar.

Features

  • Shows the current calendar event and its progress
  • Shows the next event when nothing is currently running
  • Expands on hover to show today's calendar events and reminders
  • Displays all-day events and reminders clearly
  • Uses your calendar colors for visual progress

Privacy

CalBar reads your Calendar and Reminders data locally through Apple's EventKit framework. Your calendar and reminder data never leaves your device.

CalBar does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.
