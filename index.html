CalBar Support

CalBar is a lightweight macOS menu bar app that shows the progress of your current Calendar event. It helps you stay aware of meetings, focused work blocks, classes, and reminders without opening Calendar.

Features

  • Shows the current calendar event and its progress
  • Shows the next event when nothing is currently running
  • Expands on hover to show today's calendar events and reminders
  • Displays all-day events and reminders clearly
  • Uses your calendar colors for visual progress
  • Works quietly as a macOS menu bar app

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does CalBar ask for Calendar access?

CalBar needs Calendar access to display your current and upcoming events in the menu bar.

Why does CalBar ask for Reminders access?

CalBar uses Reminders access only to show today's reminders together with your calendar schedule.

Does CalBar upload my calendar or reminder data?

No. CalBar reads Calendar and Reminders data locally on your Mac through Apple's EventKit framework. It does not upload your calendar or reminder data to any server.

Does CalBar use analytics or tracking?

No. CalBar does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.
