# CalBar Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

CalBar respects your privacy.

## Data Collection

CalBar does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.

## Calendar and Reminders Access

CalBar requests access to your Calendar and Reminders only to display your current calendar event, today's schedule, and today's reminders inside the app.

This data is read locally on your Mac through Apple's EventKit framework.

CalBar does not upload your Calendar or Reminders data to any server.

## Network Access

CalBar does not use network access to transmit your personal data.
