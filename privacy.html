CalBar Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

CalBar does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

CalBar uses Apple's EventKit framework to request access to your Calendar and Reminders so it can display today's events, reminders, and event progress in the macOS menu bar area.

Calendar and Reminders data is processed locally on your Mac and never leaves your device.

CalBar does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.

CalBar does not upload your calendar events, reminders, titles, times, locations, notes, or any related information to any server.
